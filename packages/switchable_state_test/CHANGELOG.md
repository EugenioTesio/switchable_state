## 0.2.0

- Checks the `switchable_state` 0.2 contract: nullable snapshots, `isClosed`, `listen`, `StateListener`, `asListenable`, equal writes, `value` reads that must not subscribe, moving a watcher or selector to another store, writes from `initState`, and re-picking with an `==` method tear-off.
- The reference adapter moved to `switchable_state` as `ValueNotifierStateAdapter`.

## 0.1.0

- Initial release.
- `stateStoreContract`: the conformance suite every official adapter passes, for checking your own `StateAdapter`.
- `host`: wraps a widget in the text direction that `Text` needs without an app.
